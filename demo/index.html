04 / UNDERSTAND THE RESULTS

What you can learn

How prices changed

See how often waiting cost more or saved money on three routes from Baltimore.

Compare recorded fares 1, 3, or 7 days apart. Each trip counts equally.

What we cannot tell you yet

We cannot predict your next fare or guarantee the latest cheap booking day.

The histories end before departure, and some older prices were later revised.

Data coverage and limits

Departures: 10-31-2026 to 12-25-2026. Prices observed from 08-30-2026 or 08-31-2026 through 10-30-2026.

Google Flights histories via SerpApi. Search filters, taxes, and timezones are not fully verified. Missing prices stay missing; large price changes are retained.

Trips share travel seasons and market conditions, so these findings may not apply elsewhere.

Sources, reproducibility, and publication status

Saved, checksum verified data only. No credentials, live searches, or raw responses.

Source: SerpApi Price Insights documentation. Data display/redistribution permissions remain unresolved under the provider terms. This build stays local and is excluded from version control.
