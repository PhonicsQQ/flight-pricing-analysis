04 / WHAT THIS CAN TELL US

A useful study, with boundaries.

What we measured

Google Flights histories via SerpApi. Each history tracks a route and departure date, not a seat.

Departures: 10-31-2026 to 12-25-2026. Observations: 08-30-2026 or 08-31-2026 through 10-30-2026. Selected BWI routes; all histories end before departure.

Exact 1/3/7-day changes, weighted equally by trip. Percentages use the starting fare; missing values stay missing.

What remains uncertain

Historical filters, tax comparability, and timezone semantics remain unverified. Dates provisionally use America/New_York.

Large jumps are retained. Resampling keeps each trip together but cannot capture all shared market shocks.

Predictions are deferred until revised histories and future-period evaluation are addressed.

Sources, reproducibility, and publication status

Saved, checksum-verified data only. No credentials, live searches, or raw responses.

Source: SerpApi Price Insights documentation. Data display/redistribution permissions remain unresolved under the provider terms. This build stays local and is excluded from version control.
